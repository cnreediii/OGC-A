# Western Australia State Land Information Platform (SLIP)

## Summary

The goals for the Shared Land Information Platform (SLIP) as stated by the Western Australian Government are achievable. Existing land information systems and services operating in different departments and agencies can be presented to the global Internet in a coherent manner. Land information services that are more readily available and accessible will be used more frequently, thereby increasing their value to all.
SLIP aligns with the WA Department of the Premier and Cabinet’s Office of e-Government’s, e-Government Interoperability Framework (e-GIF). e-GIF has been prepared in response to the Delivering Better Government Policy (www.premier.wa.gov.au/policies/public_sector_reform.pdf) and acknowledges the Commonwealth Government’s base policies and standards (as in NOIE 2003).

SLIP is to provide an extended framework for collaboration, building on existing inter-agency and private sector initiatives, to focus on information interchange, value-added services and delivery including to those users not necessarily expert in land information but who require such information to facilitate/improve their own cost-effective levels of service delivery.
